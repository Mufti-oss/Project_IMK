## Brand & Style

This design system drives the user portal for smart campus parking infrastructure at Universitas Gunadarma. The brand posture balances modern academic prestige with high-efficiency utility: it is precise, welcoming, agile, and technically dependable. The primary interface language is Bahasa Indonesia across all interaction layers, metadata labels, status chips, and contextual alerts.

### Target Audience & Emotional Arc
- **Civitas Academica (Students, Faculty, Administrative Staff, Visitors):** Drivers navigating tight academic schedules who require real-time clarity, rapid bay location, secure digital permits, and transparent payment/access validation.
- **Emotional Response:** Effortless control, calm assurance during peak transit hours, and a high-trust feeling of interacting with a tier-one smart university infrastructure.

### Design Style & Visual Direction
The design direction adopts a **Modern SaaS Campus Portal** aesthetic. It leverages clean structural whitespace, crisp dual-tone card containers, functional emerald-amber-rose telemetry signals, and refined indigo-purple brand anchors. Glass textures are constrained strictly to persistent navigation floats and elevated modal backdrops; day-to-day interactions prioritize immediate glanceability, soft-elevation container surfaces, and rigorous typography hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette balances institutional identity with real-time operational state signifiers. The canvas remains bright, clean, and legible under high-glare mobile environments.

### Core Brand Tokens
- **Primary Brand (`#7C3AED`):** Interactive focal points, active states, prime navigation accents, and primary action buttons.
- **Deep Purple (`#5B21B6`):** Hover/press targets, high-emphasis headers, active segment controls, and branded banners.
- **Light Purple (`#EDE9FE`):** Soft focus rings, selected card fills, subtle icon badges, and active tab indicator backdrops.

### Status & Telemetry Accents (Sistem Ketersediaan Parkir)
- **Status Tersedia (`#10B981`):** Indicates open parking spots, successful barrier scans, valid permits, and positive balances.
- **Status Hampir Penuh / Perhatian (`#F59E0B`):** Warns of limited bay availability (<15%), expiring parking sessions, or pending validations.
- **Status Penuh / Terisi (`#EF4444`):** Flags zero-capacity zones, barrier lockouts, expired permits, and emergency restrictions.

### Surface & Neutral Architecture
- **App Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** Base slate backdrop providing clear separation against pure white cards.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity primary containment layer.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E8F0`):** Soft structural hairpins separating data grids and card boundaries.
- **Typography Neutrals:** Headings and primary text utilize deep slate (`#0F172A`); secondary captions, meta tags, and passive states utilize muted slate (`#334155` and `#64748B`).

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric personality of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines, quantitative KPI metrics, badge counters, and action labels, with the high-legibility mechanics of **Inter** for dense transactional tables, data feeds, and explanatory body copy.

### Hierarchical Guidelines
- **Telemetry & Stats Counters:** Use `headline-lg` or `display-lg` rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans 700/800 for instant glanceability of remaining parking spaces (e.g., "142 Slot Tersedia").
- **Strict Indonesian Localization:** All semantic microcopy must maintain proper sentence capitalization (e.g., *Riwayat Parkir*, *Lokasi Gedung*, *Kendaraan Terdaftar*, *Pindai Tiket Masuk*).
- **Tabular Numerics:** For real-time slot tallies, vehicle plate numbers, and transaction totals, activate monospace numeric figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within Inter to prevent horizontal layout shift during dynamic refresh events.

## Layout & Spacing

The portal is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system with strict max-width constraints on ultra-wide viewports to maintain ergonomic tracking distance.

### Breakpoints & Grid Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** Max container width 1280px or 1440px. 12 columns, 24px (`space-lg`) gutters, 32px canvas margins. Left-anchored persistent campus navigation rail (260px fixed width).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, 16px (`space-md`) gutters, 24px margins. Navigation collapses to an icon-rail or top bar. Zone metrics switch to 2-column card arrays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px outer margin. Layout stacks into a single vertical stream with sticky bottom operational navigation for QR gate pass access.

### Spacing Principles
- Component padding standardizes on `space-md` (16px) for compact widgets, `space-lg` (24px) for metric overview cards, and `space-xl` (32px) for canvas section separation.
- Vertical rhythm follows multiples of 4px/8px to align iconography, badge heights, and form rows seamlessly.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs a multi-tiered surface system composed of pure white containers resting on slate backdrops, enclosed by hairline borders and ambient, non-muddy shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Kanvas Dasar):** `#F8FAFC` flat surface. No shadow, no border.
- **Level 1 (Kartu & Panel Standar):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0` and `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` (`shadow-sm`). Used for parking zone summaries, vehicle list entries, and historical logs.
- **Level 2 (Kartu Interaktif & Status Aktif):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `border: 1px solid #CBD5E1` and `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` (`shadow-md`). Applied during card hover, selected parking zone focus, and active QR pass cards.
- **Level 3 (Modal, Dropdown, & Menu Melayang):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)` (`shadow-xl`). Used for barcode gate-scanning modals, spot reservation dialogs, and campus switcher menus.
- **Level 4 (Floating Action Bar / Sticky Gate Access):** Backdrop blur `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, semi-transparent surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`, border `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`, and `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.1)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric curvature tuned to Level 2 (`roundedness: 2`), ensuring interactive components feel approachable while preserving high data density.

### Radius Scale
- **Small Controls (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem - 8px):** Input text fields, search boxes, dropdown triggers, and sub-action buttons.
- **Primary Cards & Containers (`rounded-xl` / 0.75rem to 1rem - 12px to 16px):** Metric dashboard cards, vehicle profile cards, real-time campus capacity monitors, and table panels.
- **Hero Modules & QR Enclosures (`rounded-2xl` / 1rem to 1.5rem - 16px to 24px):** Main digital gate entry pass (`Tiket Masuk Digital`), hero campus capacity view, and payment completion banners.
- **Badges & Pill Elements (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Live availability indicators, status chips (*Tersedia*, *Hampir Penuh*, *Penuh*), and quick filter tabs.

## Components

All components are engineered to deliver glanceable operational status with zero ambiguity.

### Tombol (Buttons)
- **Primary (`Tombol Utama`):** Background `#7C3AED`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded 8px–10px, bold font weight. Active/hover transitions to `#5B21B6`. Focus ring is 3px solid `#EDE9FE`.
- **Secondary (`Tombol Sekunder`):** Background `#EDE9FE`, text `#5B21B6`, hover background `#DDD6FE`.
- **Outline / Netral:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#334155`. Hover state elevates with subtle shadow and border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive (`Tombol Bahaya`):** Background `#FEF2F2`, border `1px solid #FEE2E2`, text `#EF4444`. Hover switches to solid `#EF4444` with white text.

### Lencana Status (Chips & Status Badges)
Pill-shaped containers (`rounded-full`) displaying real-time telemetry with a left-aligned 6px status dot:
- **Tersedia:** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, pulse dot `#10B981`.
- **Hampir Penuh:** Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, solid dot `#F59E0B`.
- **Penuh:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, solid dot `#EF4444`.
- **Informasi / Kampus:** Background `#EDE9FE`, text `#5B21B6` (e.g., *Kampus D*, *Kampus E*, *Kampus J*).

### Kartu Slot & Kapasitas (Capacity & Spot Cards)
Surface `#FFFFFF` with `border: 1px solid #E2E8F0` and `rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl`. Features an upper header row with building/zone identifier (*Gedung 4 - Parkir Motor*), a central occupancy metric rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` 32px, and a dual-tone capacity progress bar (track `#F1F5F9`, indicator dynamically mapped to primary `#7C3AED` or status colors).

### Bidang Input (Form Fields & Search)
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, height 42px.
- Focus state applies border `#7C3AED` with an outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.15)`.
- License plate lookup input features uppercase formatting with monospace letter spacing (`font-mono tracking-widest`).

### Kartu Akses QR & Tiket Masuk (Gate Pass Module)
Dedicated high-contrast card featuring clean white surface, rounded-2xl geometry, subtle dashed divider line representing perforation, and centered high-density QR code for barrier scanners. Below the barcode: plate number display (*Nomor Plat: B 1234 UGD*), timestamp (*Waktu Masuk*), and active status badge (*Sesi Aktif*).

### Navigasi & Daftar (Navigation & List Items)
Lists utilize alternating row separation via hairline borders (`#F1F5F9`). Active sidebar items highlight with `#EDE9FE` background, `#7C3AED` text, and a 3px vertical left accent bar in `#7C3AED`.
